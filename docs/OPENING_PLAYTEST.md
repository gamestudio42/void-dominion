# First-time-player opening test

Milestone 9 remains open until five people who have not played this game complete the sessions below. No participants have been tested for this milestone yet. Browser automation is separate evidence and cannot fill these records.

## Hypothesis and procedure

Direct command: a new commander can select a worker, complete a barracks, produce a rifleman before first contact, and explain that losing every command center causes defeat. Keep the simulation, costs and Varek plans unchanged during this first cohort.

1. Record the exact commit/build, browser, viewport, input device and locale. Start a fresh normal skirmish with opening goals enabled. Record whether the player dismisses them. Use easy/hard only for separately labelled follow-up sessions.
2. Explain only how to open the game. Do not coach selection, building placement, production or defense. Record requests for help and the point where the player gets stuck.
3. Record match-clock times for the first completed barracks, first completed rifleman and first visible enemy unit (Contact). Also record elapsed wall time separately if useful; software rendering can slow simulation time.
4. Continue to victory/defeat. Save the replay, record the result, and ask the player to explain what caused it. Keep their actual words separate from the observer's interpretation.
5. Count success only when both opening units/buildings are complete before contact without verbal coaching. Retain failed attempts and skipped guidance in the cohort.

Proposed acceptance: at least four of five players meet the opening criterion and correctly identify what must be defended. If fewer do, identify one observed source of confusion, revise it and repeat. Do not rebalance combat from these five sessions alone. If guidance obscures an essential control or creates confusion, revise its placement/copy before retaining it.
